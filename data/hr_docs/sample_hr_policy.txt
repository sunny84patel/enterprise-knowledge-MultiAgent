EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK — HR POLICIES
Version 3.2 | Effective January 2024

=== SECTION 1: EMPLOYMENT TERMS ===

1.1 Notice Period
All permanent employees are required to provide a minimum notice period of 30 days
before resignation. Senior roles (Team Lead and above) require 60 days notice.
Notice must be submitted in writing to the direct manager and HR department.

1.2 Probation Period
New employees serve a probation period of 90 days. During probation, either party
may terminate employment with 7 days written notice. Extension of probation (up to
30 additional days) may be granted at management discretion.

1.3 Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Flexible working
arrangements must be approved by the line manager and HR. Remote work is permitted
up to 3 days per week for eligible roles.

=== SECTION 2: LEAVE POLICY ===

2.1 Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues at 1.67 days per month. Unused leave up to 5 days may be carried forward
to the next year. Leave encashment is not permitted except upon separation.

2.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate
is required for absences exceeding 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry forward.

2.3 Maternity/Paternity Leave
Maternity leave: 26 weeks paid for the first two children, 12 weeks for subsequent children.
Paternity leave: 15 days paid leave, to be availed within 6 months of the child's birth.

2.4 Emergency Leave
Up to 3 days of paid emergency leave may be granted for immediate family bereavement
or medical emergencies. Additional unpaid leave may be approved at management discretion.

=== SECTION 3: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT ===

3.1 Performance Reviews
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice annually: mid-year (July) and year-end
(December). Reviews follow the OKR framework. Employees receive ratings from 1 (Below
Expectations) to 5 (Exceptional).

3.2 Performance Improvement Plans
Employees rated 1 or 2 for two consecutive reviews will be placed on a Performance
Improvement Plan (PIP) of 60 days. Failure to meet PIP targets may result in termination.

3.3 Promotions
Promotion cycles run annually in January. Employees must have a minimum rating of 4
for two consecutive reviews and manager endorsement to be considered.

=== SECTION 4: DATA RETENTION POLICY ===

4.1 Employee Records
All employee records including contracts, payroll, and performance data must be retained
for a minimum of 7 years from the date of separation as per statutory requirements.

4.2 Customer Data
Customer personal data must be retained only as long as necessary for the stated purpose,
with a maximum retention period of 5 years unless legally required otherwise.

4.3 Data Deletion
Upon expiry of the retention period, data must be securely deleted using approved methods.
Physical documents must be shredded. Digital data must be overwritten or cryptographically
erased. A deletion certificate must be filed with the Data Protection Officer.

=== SECTION 5: CODE OF CONDUCT ===

5.1 Workplace Behaviour
All employees must maintain a respectful, inclusive workplace. Harassment, discrimination,
or bullying of any kind will result in immediate disciplinary action up to and including
termination.

5.2 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any potential conflict of interest in writing to HR within 5
business days of becoming aware of it. Undisclosed conflicts may result in disciplinary action.

5.3 Confidentiality
All employees must maintain strict confidentiality of company information, client data,
and business strategies both during and for 2 years after their employment period.
