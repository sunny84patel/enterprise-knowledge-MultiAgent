LEGAL SERVICES AGREEMENT — STANDARD TERMS
Document Reference: LSA-2024-001

=== CLAUSE 1: ENGAGEMENT AND SCOPE ===

1.1 Scope of Services
The firm agrees to provide legal advisory services as detailed in Schedule A attached
hereto. Services include contract review, compliance advisory, and dispute resolution
consultation. Any services outside this scope require a separate written engagement.

1.2 Retainer Fee
A monthly retainer of INR 50,000 is payable in advance on the 1st of each month.
The retainer covers up to 10 hours of advisory time per month. Additional hours are
billed at INR 8,000 per hour.

=== CLAUSE 2: COMPLAINT FILING PROCEDURE ===

2.1 Filing a Complaint
All legal complaints must be filed using Form L-101, available on the firm's client
portal. Complaints must be submitted within 60 days of the incident giving rise to
the complaint. Late filings will not be accepted except in cases of force majeure.

2.2 Complaint Review Process
Upon receipt, complaints are acknowledged within 2 business days. A preliminary review
is completed within 10 business days. Complex matters may require up to 30 business
days for a full response.

2.3 Escalation
If the initial response is unsatisfactory, clients may escalate to the Senior Partner
within 15 days of receiving the initial response. Final escalation decisions are binding.

=== CLAUSE 3: CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION ===

3.1 Attorney-Client Privilege
All communications between the client and firm are protected by attorney-client privilege.
The firm will not disclose client information to any third party without explicit written
consent, except as required by law or court order.

3.2 Data Retention
Client files and correspondence are retained for a minimum of 7 years post-engagement
in accordance with Bar Council regulations. Electronic records are stored on encrypted
servers with access logging.

=== CLAUSE 4: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY ===

4.1 Cap on Liability
The firm's total liability in connection with any matter shall not exceed the total fees
paid by the client in the 12 months preceding the claim.

4.2 Exclusions
The firm shall not be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages. The firm
is not liable for outcomes arising from incomplete or inaccurate information provided
by the client.

=== CLAUSE 5: TERMINATION ===

5.1 Termination by Client
The client may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice. Fees for work
completed up to the termination date remain payable.

5.2 Termination by Firm
The firm may terminate the engagement immediately if the client engages in conduct
that violates applicable laws, fails to pay outstanding invoices within 45 days, or
places the firm in an ethical conflict.
